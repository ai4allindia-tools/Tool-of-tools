🔒 Privacy Policy

Tool of Tools is designed around browser-based utilities. Where supported, files and text are processed locally in your browser rather than intentionally uploaded to our server.


We do not ask you to enter passwords, payment information or other sensitive personal information into these tools.


Some features use third-party libraries loaded through content delivery networks. Advertising, analytics or other third-party services may be added later and this policy should then be updated.
